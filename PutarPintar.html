02 — Auto-detect revolutions

Drag a small box on the video over the spot the mass crosses each revolution (a high-contrast point works best). Then run Auto-detect — it plays through the video once and finds every crossing for you.

Click roughly on the center of the circular path, then drag out to the mass itself — this is just a starting guess. The tracker follows the mass frame-by-frame from there and fits the real path afterward, so it copes with hand shake, an off-center guess, or a path that isn't a perfect circle.
Tip: for outdoor or busy-background footage, trim FROM/TO to a short, clean 2–3 second stretch (roughly 5–8 revolutions) rather than the whole clip — a shorter, uncluttered segment tracks far more reliably than one long clip with people or traffic moving in the background.
